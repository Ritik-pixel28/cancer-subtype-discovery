RESEARCH EXPLORER / 03GOLUB LEUKEMIA · G87

A considered preparation.

Understand feature variability and the transformation from original expression measurements to a comparable numerical representation.

Feature selection: MAD

All 7,129 probes are ranked by their median absolute deviation (MAD). The top 2,000 probes are retained for downstream analysis. High variability does not imply biological importance or cancer specificity.

Standardization

Each selected probe is centered (mean subtracted) and divided by its population standard deviation (ddof=0). This creates a gene-wise z-score representation. No cancer labels enter this transformation.

Negative values

The original Golub expression measurements contain negative values (147,174 negative entries). A naive log2(x+1) transform is not applied because it cannot handle negative input.

RESEARCH EXPLORER / 10GOLUB LEUKEMIA · G87

Understand the surrogate.

Explainability artifacts from a surrogate classifier trained to reproduce cluster assignments.

Explainability artifacts are not available in this deployment.

The explainability module requires a surrogate classifier (RandomForestClassifier, Phase 8) to be trained and its SHAP values exported. The following files would be required:

reports/explainability/global_importance.csv — per-cluster mean |SHAP|
reports/explainability/sample_explanations.csv — sample-level SHAP contributions
reports/explainability/surrogate_predictions.csv — surrogate vs consensus labels
reports/explainability/model_metadata.json — model provenance

These files were not generated in the current research pipeline. The explainability section is correctly shown as pending.
What would be shown

Global SHAP importance

Top features contributing to each cluster's surrogate prediction, ranked by mean absolute SHAP value.

Sample-level explanations

Per-sample SHAP contributions showing which probes pushed a sample toward or away from each cluster.

Surrogate fidelity

Agreement between the surrogate classifier's predictions and the original consensus assignments. This measures how reproducible the clusters are, not clinical accuracy.

Scientific limitations (always present)
This study uses a small cohort (n=72). Cluster discovery and biomarker candidates were identified in the same dataset with no independent validation cohort. All findings are exploratory and hypothesis-generating only. The k=3 consensus solution is not unique; other values of k are plausible. External agreement (ARI) is modest and varies by algorithm. No clinical or prognostic associations are established or implied.
Molecular animation provenance

The homepage molecular visualization in the original Streamlit application renders 486 DNA atoms from the published 1BNA B-DNA dodecamer (Drew et al., 1981, doi:10.1073/pnas.78.4.2179). Atomic coordinates are from RCSB PDB 1BNA. Colors, lighting, and camera motion are artistic. This is a structural reference, not a cancer-specific gene or patient sequence.
